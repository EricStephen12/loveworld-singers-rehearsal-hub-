## 🎨 Color Scheme

### **Backgrounds**
- Main: `#1a1a1a`
- Cards: `gradient from-[#2a2a2a] to-[#252525]`
- Inputs: `#3a3a3a`

### **Accents**
- Primary: `purple-500` to `purple-700`
- Record: `red-500` to `red-600`
- Icons: `purple-400`
- Text: `white` (headings), `gray-300` (body), `gray-400` (labels)

### **Effects**
- Shadows: `shadow-lg`, `shadow-xl`
- Glows: `shadow-purple-500/30`, `shadow-red-500/50`
- Borders: `border-white/10`, `border-purple-500/30`
- Gradients: `from-purple-600 to-purple-700`
